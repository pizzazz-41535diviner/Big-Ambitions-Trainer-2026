## 🎮 About the Game

Big Ambitions is a single-player role-playing business simulation and life-management game developed by Hovgaard Games. It entered Early Access on March 10, 2023, and received its full release on August 28, 2026. Set in New York City, the game lets players build businesses, manage employees, handle logistics, invest in property and funds, and develop their character's lifestyle.

## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **Big Ambitions**. This trainer provides quality-of-life controls for Big Ambitions' business, financial, character, inventory, vehicle, and time-management systems. It is designed to reduce repetitive tasks while preserving the game's core business sandbox and progression structure.

**Target Audience:** Players who want to experiment with business layouts, test logistics networks, build large company groups, explore the city freely, reduce repetitive management tasks, or create custom sandbox scenarios without changing the game's overall business-simulation concept.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Big Ambitions uses direct character movement through a fully explorable New York environment. The player can walk through properties, travel by public transport, and drive owned vehicles. Use the vehicle fuel and condition options when long-distance travel or repeated deliveries become tedious. Time controls are useful when moving between locations or waiting for scheduled business activity.

### Tools & Inventory

Inventory is important for personal supplies, purchased goods, deliveries, and business restocking. Use the inventory editor only after opening the relevant inventory screen, then adjust the quantity of the selected item. Avoid changing items during active transfers, loading, unloading, or delivery transactions to reduce the risk of save inconsistencies.

### World & Environment

The city supports business ownership, property purchases, shopping, dealerships, suppliers, warehouses, offices, and transportation. Time affects sleep, employee schedules, deliveries, operating hours, expenses, and other daily systems. Use cash and time options to test locations, layouts, investment plans, logistics chains, and different business strategies.

### Combat & Weapons

Big Ambitions has no combat gameplay. There are no weapons, enemies, combat encounters, health-based battles, or combat progression systems. The trainer therefore provides no combat-related functions.
